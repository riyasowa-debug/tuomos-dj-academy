Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I'm TUOMOS. I perform in Toronto clubs and teach aspiring DJs how to mix professionally.

My goal is simple: help you become confident behind the decks faster than learning alone.

Learning Stages

Stage 1

Foundation

Build a strong foundation and feel confident behind the decks.

  • DJ equipment (CDJs, Mixer & Rekordbox)
  • Buttons & Controls
  • Beatmatching with Sync
  • Track Structure & Phrasing
  • EQ Mixing
  • Your First Transitions
  • Building Your First DJ Set
Duration: 6 Hours

Stage 2

Performance

Move beyond the basics and start playing with confidence.

  • Beatmatching without Sync
  • Headphone Cueing
  • Advanced Transitions
  • Effects (FX)
  • Energy Flow
  • Playlist Preparation
  • Creating a Complete DJ Set
Duration: 8 Hours

Stage 3

Mentorship

Develop your own style and learn what separates good DJs from great ones.

  • Reading the Crowd
  • Song Selection
  • Storytelling Through Music
  • Building & Controlling Energy
  • Finding Your Own Style
  • Club Preparation
  • Industry Insights
  • Personal Feedback & Set Analysis
Duration: 11 Hours
